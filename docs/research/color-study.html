Color changes have a route

Compare the same endpoints through four mixing methods. Move the slider to inspect a moment in the fade. Everything is static until you change a control.

This is a display study in sRGB/CSS. It does not predict a lamp's spectrum, dimmer curve, brightness or the appearance of a person under it.

Questions to study: Does the middle become unexpectedly dark or neutral? Does the route preserve the intended palette? Is a neutral midpoint useful here? Try white → white and inspect the last row.

The current saturated mixer intentionally subtracts the shared RGB component. That prevents pale and neutral colors, so it is unsuitable as a general-purpose stage color mixer. A designed transition may legitimately pass through white.

Oklab uses the browser's CSS implementation and gamut handling. The linear-light row clamps to the sRGB display range. Neither supplies fixture calibration. Your monitor and viewing conditions also affect the result.
